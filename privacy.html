Privacy Policy

AI Powered Tactics · Last updated 10 October 2026

AI Powered Tactics helps small-sided football teams plan line-ups, run sub rotations, record scores and stats, and make highlight reels. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

The app is run by Ethan Davison-Pearce. For anything about your data, email ethan@reth.com.

What we keep

InformationWhoseWhy
Email address and passwordAnyone who creates an accountTo sign in, link you to your player, and let gaffers manage their teams. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our database provider.
Your answers to game polls (in, in goal or out) and your playing positionPlayersSo the team can see who's playing the next game and where.
Last name, if you add one, and which player on a team is youPlayers with an accountTo show you your own stats. Your last name is shown only to you and your team's gaffers.
Team details: name, colours, crest, settings, follow and gaffer codesTeamsTo run the team in the app and let followers find it by code.
Players' names or nicknames, as typed in by an gafferPlayersTo plan line-ups and show stats.
Match records: opponents, scores, minutes played, goals, assists, saves and the times they happenedTeams and playersTo show live games, stats and season totals, and to build highlight reels.
Goals logged by followers, when a team allows itTeamsPassed to the gaffer's app and then deleted.
Highlight clips: short videos cut from a match recording on a gaffer's phone, with their captionsTeams and playersSo the team can watch highlights by game and by player. The full recording stays on the gaffer's phone and is not uploaded.

Following a team needs no account, and the app keeps nothing about followers who don't sign up.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • No location, contacts or microphone access.
  • Full match recordings never leave your devices. Only the short highlight clips a gaffer chooses to cut are uploaded.

Where it's stored

Team and account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in its EU (Ireland) region. Each phone also keeps a copy of the teams it has opened so the app works without signal; that copy stays on the phone. The iOS app's change alerts are scheduled on the phone itself and are not sent through any server.

Who can see team data

Anyone with a team's 6-character code can see that team's name, crest, players' names, live games, stats and highlight clips. Clip files are stored at web addresses that are not listed anywhere but can be opened by anyone who has the link. Only the team's gaffers can change anything. Gaffers decide which names to enter, so a nickname works just as well as a real name.

How long we keep it

Data is kept until an gaffer removes it or deletes their account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. This removes your email and sign-in straight away. Teams you run on your own are deleted with their games and stats; teams with other gaffers carry on without you, and your player becomes unclaimed.
  • Players can ask their team's gaffer to rename or remove them, or email us.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing ethan@reth.com. If you're in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, which in the UK is the ICO.

Children

Accounts are for adults. Teams with younger players should be run by an adult gaffer, who decides which names are entered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
