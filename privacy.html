Privacy Policy

Gaffer · Last updated 18 October 2026

Gaffer helps small-sided football teams plan line-ups, run sub rotations, record scores and stats, and make highlight reels. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it. The rules for using Gaffer are in the Terms of use.

Who is responsible

The app is run by Ethan Davison-Pearce. For anything about your data, email ethan@reth.com.

What we don't do

  • No banner ads, analytics or selling of data, and nothing is shared for marketing. The only ads are the ones you choose to watch on the free plan (see the table above).
  • No contacts access. The camera and microphone are used only when a gaffer chooses to film a game with their iPhone in Gaffer: the picture and sound go to the live stream (see the table above), and the full recording is saved to that iPhone's own Photos, nowhere else. The microphone is also used when someone chooses to record their own commentary over a highlight reel: the recording stays on that iPhone and goes only into the reel they make, which is stored with Gaffer only if they put it on a player's card. Location is used only if a gaffer taps “Use where I am now” to set the team's home ground; nobody's own whereabouts are stored or tracked.
  • Nothing is read from or saved to Apple Health. During a game the Apple Watch app starts a football workout only so it stays on screen when you raise your wrist; it ends at full time and isn't saved.
  • Full match recordings never leave your devices. Only the short highlight clips a gaffer chooses to cut are uploaded. (A game a gaffer chooses to stream live is the exception: see the table above.)

Where it's stored

Team and account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in its EU (Ireland) region. Highlight clips, and live streams and their recordings, are stored by Cloudflare. AI commentary lines go to Google Cloud to be turned into speech (see the table above). Each of these providers handles the data only to run Gaffer and protects it at least as well as this policy says. Each phone also keeps a copy of the teams it has opened so the app works without signal; that copy stays on the phone. The iOS app's change alerts are scheduled on the phone itself and are not sent through any server.

Who can see team data

Anyone with a team's 6-character code can see that team's name, crest, players' names, live games, stats and highlight clips, and watch a game the gaffer is streaming live. Clip files are stored at web addresses that are not listed anywhere but can be opened by anyone who has the link. The squad chat is different: it can be read only by the team's signed-in players and gaffers. Only the team's gaffers can change anything. Gaffers decide which names to enter, so a nickname works just as well as a real name.

Anyone signed in can also find a team by name and look at its squad's names, results, players' totals and highlight clips, unless the team's gaffer switches off "Let people find this team" in Options. This never shows the team's code, upcoming games, the squad chat or guests' names; to follow or join a team you still need its code from the gaffer.

A goal a team's gaffer puts up for Goal of the Week is seen by anyone signed in, with the team's name, until it's taken down; the winning goal and its reel stay up. Nothing else about the team is shown with it.

Profiles are separate from teams: your username and profile name are seen by anyone signed in who searches for you. Your teams, results, stats and chosen clips are seen by anyone signed in if your profile is public, or only by followers you've accepted if it's private. You can change this at any time in Profile → Edit profile, and remove any follower.

How long we keep it

Data is kept until a gaffer removes it or deletes their account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → the gear (Settings) → Your account → Delete my account (or, with no team open, Account → Delete my account). This removes your email and sign-in straight away, with your username, profile, posts, comments, likes, follows and tags. Teams you run on your own are deleted with their games and stats; teams with other gaffers carry on without you, and your player becomes unclaimed.
  • Players can ask their team's gaffer to rename or remove them, or email us.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing ethan@reth.com. If you're in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, which in the UK is the ICO.

Children

Accounts are for adults. Teams with younger players should be run by an adult gaffer, who decides which names are entered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
