Auto highlights

Film the whole game and the app works out which bits to keep. No scrubbing through footage afterwards.

  1. Film itMount a GoPro or prop up a phone and leave it rolling. One continuous video of the whole game works best; one per half is fine too.
  2. Tap as it happensEvery goal, assist and keeper save is timestamped the moment you log it. Mark anything else worth keeping with one tap, or from your Apple Watch.
  3. Line it up onceAfter the game, tell the app when kick-off appears in the video. That one number places every clip.
  4. Cut it on your phonePick the video in the iPhone app. It cuts each moment, adds the scorer and the score, and joins the clips into one reel to share.

What you get

  • A reel for every matchGoals, saves and marked moments, in order, with captions.
  • A reel for every playerAt the end of the season, each player's goals, assists and saves in one video.
  • YouTube chaptersTimestamps to paste under a full-match upload.

The cutting runs on your own computer with ffmpeg, which is free, so your footage is never uploaded anywhere.
