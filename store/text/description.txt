Gaffer runs your small-sided football team: who's playing, who's on next, the score, the stats and the highlights. Fives, sevens and everything in between.

BEFORE THE GAME
• Post the next game and let the squad say they're in, in goal or out
• A nudge for anyone who hasn't answered
• Add it to your calendar in one tap

ON MATCH DAY
• Pick who's playing and Gaffer plans the subs, from fair (everyone plays about the same) to going for the win
• A match clock that counts down to each change and tells you who's coming off
• Log goals, assists, saves and highlights with one tap, and the whole squad can follow the score
• Works with no signal at the pitch
• On an Apple Watch too: run the clock, feel a tap when subs are due and log goals from your wrist

AFTER THE FINAL WHISTLE
• Highlight reels cut on your iPhone from your GoPro or iPhone footage: every goal with the score, your badge and the match clock, like football on the telly
• Optional AI commentary over your reels
• Stats, form, match ratings, man of the match and who plays well together
• Share reels with the squad, or post your best moments

FOR THE WHOLE SQUAD
• Squad chat
• Player profiles with goals, assists and pinned clips
• Follow your teammates, and find other teams

Built by a gaffer who runs his own team with it every week.
