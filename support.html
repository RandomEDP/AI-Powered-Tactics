Support

Gaffer: 5-a-Side Team Manager

Stuck, found a bug, or want something added? Email ethan@reth.com and you'll get a reply from the person who makes the app.

Joining a team

Ask your gaffer for the team's 6-character code. Enter it on the first screen, pick your name, choose your position and create an account. If your name is greyed out, someone has already claimed it: ask your gaffer to free it up.

Starting a team

Tap "Create a team", create an account, then add your team's name, colour and players. Share the code that appears so the squad can join.

I can't start a game or change the options

Only gaffers can. A gaffer can make you one too by sharing the gaffer code from Options.

Notifications aren't arriving

You need to be signed in, to have claimed your player, and to have allowed notifications for Gaffer in your iPhone's Settings.

The Apple Watch app says "Start a game on your iPhone"

The watch shows the game that is running on the gaffer's iPhone. Start the game there and keep the phone nearby.

Deleting your account

In the app: your account → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is removed.
